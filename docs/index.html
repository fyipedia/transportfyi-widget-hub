FAQ

How many airports does AirportFYI cover?

AirportFYI covers 4,500+ airports with IATA (3-letter) + ICAO (4-letter) codes, geographic coordinates, runway data, elevation, time zone, operating airlines.

What aircraft data does PlaneFYI provide?

PlaneFYI covers Airbus, Boeing, Embraer, Bombardier, ATR. Specifications: wingspan, length, MTOW, capacity, range, engines, first flight, family variants.

Which airlines does AirlineFYI cover?

AirlineFYI covers major + regional airlines with IATA + ICAO codes. Alliances (Star Alliance, oneworld, SkyTeam), hub airports, fleet composition.

What railway data does TrainFYI provide?

TrainFYI covers railway stations, train routes, rail networks. High-speed rail (Shinkansen, KTX, TGV, ICE, HSR China). Station codes, electrification, gauge.

Why a family of 4 sites?

Each Transport FYI site has dedicated query patterns and cross-references (airline hub airports, aircraft used by carriers, multi-modal trains+flights). Specialization enables deeper SEO and richer linking.
